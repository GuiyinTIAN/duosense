THE DUOSENSE CONCEPT

Spatial detail.
Motion context.

Two complementary sensing methods, carried by a common rotating assembly. The goal: interpret a person’s presence and activity using geometry and motion together.

01 / GEOMETRY

LiDAR maps the space

Distance measurements describe position, shape and height. Sweeping a single-line scanning plane expands coverage beyond one fixed cross-section.

Current LiDAR prototype
02 / MOTION

Radar adds motion cues

The proposed radar processing uses velocity, Doppler and micro-Doppler features to complement the spatial contours measured by LiDAR.

Fusion development goal
03 / COVERAGE

Two axes, one assembly

The LiDAR scans internally while an external actuator rotates the sensing assembly. “Dual-axis” refers to these two scanning motions—not two external motors.

Rotational sensing architecture

The proposed fusion is intended to help distinguish human activity from changes caused by furniture or other objects. No measured recognition accuracy or false-alarm reduction is claimed here.

What should I know about the current limitations? +

Posture estimates use baseline differences and geometric rules, not a validated human recognition model. Occlusion, furniture, multiple people, and mounting tilt can affect results. Automatic 2D ground calibration compensates height offset, not tilt. A transition to low posture can indicate a suspected fall, but crouching can produce similar observations. This prototype is not a certified safety or medical alarm.

RESEARCH DIRECTIONS

Designed around
private indoor spaces.

Target applications described in the system design—not validated deployments or available alarm services.

Bedrooms & bedside areas

Explore bed-exit events, nighttime movement and prolonged floor-level presence without conventional video.

Privacy-sensitive care spaces

Study posture changes, suspected falls and unusual stationary periods where camera monitoring is undesirable.

Corridors & shared areas

Investigate walking, extended stops and activity patterns through combined spatial and motion features.

Camera-free sensing does not make occupancy and activity data non-sensitive. Access, storage and deployment suitability still need careful evaluation. Wet-area or safety-critical use requires separate validation.
